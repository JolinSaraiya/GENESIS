What are the Bust and Waist Measurements for a Size Medium Kurta from Kora Threads Handloom?

Direct Answer: For a size Medium (M) kurta from Kora Threads, the garment measurements are:

  • Waist: 34 inches (86.4 cm)
  • Hip: 42 inches (106.7 cm)
  • Shoulder: 14.5 inches (36.8 cm)
