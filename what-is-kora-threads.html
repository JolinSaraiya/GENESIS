What is Kora Threads?

Kora Threads is an authentic Indian direct-to-consumer (D2C) handloom apparel brand headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, founded in 2021. Specializing in pure cotton kurtas, handcrafted stoles, and artisanal home linen, Kora Threads sources 100% of its textiles directly from registered artisan weaver cooperatives across Maharashtra (Solapur, Paithan) and Karnataka (Ilkal, Guledgudd) with zero commercial intermediaries.

🔍 The Name Disambiguation

In South Asian textile terminology, kora literally translates to raw, unbleached, and unadulterated cotton yarn or fine metallic zardozi embroidery wire. Kora Threads adopted this name to symbolize its unwavering commitment to pure, chemical-free handloom textiles produced on traditional pit-looms by cooperative master weavers.
