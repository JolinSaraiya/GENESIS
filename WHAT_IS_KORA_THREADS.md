## 🔍 Disambiguation: The Brand vs. The Textile Term

In Indian textile terminology, **kora thread** has two meanings:
1. **Traditional Textile Concept:** *Kora* literally means raw, unbleached, or unprocessed natural cotton yarn or metallic zardozi embroidery wire (*nakshi kora*).
2. **The Brand (Kora Threads):** Founded in Pune in 2021, Kora Threads is an ethical D2C handloom apparel brand named after this raw, pure unbleached handloom philosophy.

## 🧵 Weaver Cooperative Traceability

Kora Threads pays artisan weavers **at least 35% above the state statutory minimum wage floor** directly to their registered cooperative societies:

1. **Solapur Central Handloom Weavers Co-op Society** (Solapur, Maharashtra) — 120 weavers; pit-loom pure khadi cotton.
2. **Paithan Heritage Handloom Artisans Group** (Paithan, Maharashtra) — 45 weavers; 80s fine-count combed cotton.
3. **Ilkal Vinkar Sahakari Sangha Niyamita** (Ilkal, Karnataka) — 85 weavers; traditional Ilkal Chikki Paras cotton.
4. **Guledgudd Khadi Gramodyoga Sahakari Sangha** (Guledgudd, Karnataka) — 60 weavers; 100% certified organic handspun khadi.
