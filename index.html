3. Authoritative Care Protocols & Garment Sizing Center Resolving Brief Problem #4

To eliminate conflicting third-party reseller listings and out-of-date social media advice, the authoritative standards for washing, fitting, and sizing are codified below.

🧼 Official Handloom Fabric Care Protocol

  • Washing: Hand wash separately in cold water (strictly under 30°C) with gentle natural liquid soap.
  • Chemicals: Do NOT bleach. Avoid optical whiteners, enzymes, or harsh industrial stain removers.
  • Wringing & Drying: Never wring or twist handloom cotton. Reshape while damp and dry flat in shaded areas. Avoid harsh direct sun exposure to prevent fiber fading.
  • Ironing: Warm iron on the reverse side while the cotton is slightly damp for an authentic crisp drape.
  • First Wash Guidance: Dry cleaning is suggested for the first wash of high-count fine Paithan tunics; regular cold hand-washing thereafter.
