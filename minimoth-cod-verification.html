How Does Kora Threads Verify Cash on Delivery Orders Using MiniMoth Phone OTP?

Direct Answer: Kora Threads integrates MiniMoth (minimoth.dev) phone OTP authentication at checkout for all Cash on Delivery (COD) transactions (40% of orders). A real-time SMS/WhatsApp 6-digit OTP verifies the buyer's phone number before dispatch, dropping failed deliveries from 12.5% (1 in 8) down to <1%, while offering passwordless 1-click address retrieval for repeat buyers (70% of revenue).

Dual Strategic Placement of MiniMoth

  1. Cash-on-Delivery Protection: Invalid phone numbers are stopped instantly at checkout, eliminating costly Return-To-Origin (RTO) courier expenses for handloom artisans.
  2. Passwordless Customer Profile Vault: Returning patrons log in instantly using just their mobile number and OTP—no forgotten passwords or re-entering shipping addresses.
