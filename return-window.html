What is Kora Threads?

Kora Threads is an authentic Indian direct-to-consumer (D2C) handloom apparel brand headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, founded in 2021. Specializing in pure cotton kurtas, handcrafted stoles, and artisanal home linen, Kora Threads partners directly with artisan weaver cooperatives in Solapur, Paithan, Ilkal, and Guledgudd.
